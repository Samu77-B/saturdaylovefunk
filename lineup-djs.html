The radio curators who brought the groove to Broomfield Park

The radio curators

Richie Tee (Diverse FM / Jazz FM)

A powerhouse in soul radio, Richie is known for the “EnRiched” brand. His broadcasting style is built on a foundation of genuine fandom and deep industry knowledge.

Paul Lewis (Solar Radio)

With a career spanning over 40 years, Paul Lewis is a veteran of the jazz-funk trenches. Having been a resident DJ at the famous Brunel Rooms, his current “Sunday Breakfast” show on Solar Radio is a masterclass in curation, blending his encyclopedic knowledge of the past with a keen eye for modern classics.

Richard Marzetti (Solar Radio)

Marzetti has become one of Solar Radio’s most prolific voices since joining in 2012. Through his “Soul Life” and “Gospel Sunrise” shows, he explores the full spectrum of soulful music. An expert interviewer, he has sat down with legends ranging from Bluey of Incognito to Peabo Bryson, always focusing on the spirit and heart of the music.
